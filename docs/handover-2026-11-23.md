## Rules these changes add

1. **Any new saved data needs `EXP_KEYS`.** If you add a localStorage key, add it to `EXP_KEYS` **and** `EXP_OPT` in `src/game20.js`, or CI blocks the deploy. Device settings go in `DEVICE_ONLY` in `tests/check_save_keys.js`, with a reason.
2. **Never put `dustreach_cloud_v1` in `EXP_KEYS`.** It holds the device's cloud code, and an export file must never contain it.
3. **Bank:** `loadBank()` rebuilds the record field by field. A new bank field must be added there, or it is silently dropped on the next write (this is why `tabs` is listed there). Anything that moves an item out of the bank should `delete it.bt`.
4. Don't remove `window.__DR_CLOUD_API` (the test override) or the "no upload with zero characters" guard in `cloudPush`.

## Balance sweep (bot, 23 Nov)

Method: `simChar(prof, cap, rarity)` + `simStage` + `simRunFight` on each solo dungeon's final boss. 14 professions × 11 dungeons × tiers N/H/NM/A3/A5, 770 fights, plus Normal at Common/Uncommon gear.

- **Normal is on target with Uncommon gear**: median 56 s, 0.68× max HP taken. Rare gear: 36 s. Common gear: 96 s, 97% kills. That's healthy.
- **Hard-mode tiers scale cleanly**, with Rare gear: H 50 s, NM 59 s, A3 87 s, A5 104 s, and taken 0.57 → 1.59× max HP. Boss HP ratios match `HM_TIERS` exactly.
- **Fixed:** the Vault was the only outlier (see the table above). After the fix, Rare A5 kills went from 10/14 to 14/14.
- **Fixed 24 Nov (Claude): low-level energy starvation** (was open). Share of the fight with no energy to cast (Normal+NM median):
  - Vanguard: 0.26 at Choir (Lv 18), 0.24 at Stillvault (Lv 28), 0.28 at Foundry (Lv 38)
  - Flux: 0.32 at Mine (Lv 7)
  - Siege: 0.17 at Mine (Lv 7)
  - Everyone is at 0 from Heartforge (Lv 48) up. Probably Weave costs versus the energy pool before mid-levels. Worth a look before adding more low-level content.
  - Cause: Vanguard's Weave costs scale with level but its pool doesn't (spend/regen 2.7x at Lv 10, 1.8x at Lv 48); Flux's 1.6 s Lance makes it 4x; everyone else ~1.5x. Fix in `src/game49.js`: `PROF_ENR` in-combat regen bonus (Vanguard 4+0.2/lv, Flux 5+0.2/lv, Siege 2+0.05/lv), full to Lv 50, gone by Lv 70. After: starvation 0 at Choir/Stillvault/Foundry/Heartforge for all three; Flux still 0.2-0.35 at Lv 7-10 (its spam Weave is the design).
- **Stillvault (Queen Ysra) barely changes with tier** (54 s at N → 78 s at A5, while boss HP goes ×2.85): the fight is gated by phase timers, not HP. Damage taken does rise (0.9 → 1.9×). That's fine if it's intended, otherwise the Ascent tiers there are a soft touch.

## Vigil Stair sweep (bot, 23 Nov)

Method: `vgSimClimb(1, 60, 240)` for each profession at Lv 50 and Lv 100, Rare gear.

- **Fixed: crash on death in the Stair.** `game5.js` telegraph loop: a burst that kills the player ends the run, `clearTele()` empties `d.tele` mid-loop, and the next iteration read `undefined.upd` (`TypeError: Cannot read properties of undefined (reading 'upd')`). Reproduced on the old build by putting 3 lethal circles on the player. The loop now skips missing entries and doesn't splice a telegraph that was already cleared. Please keep that guard if you rework the loop.
- **Reached floors:** Lv 50: 17–39 (median ~31). Lv 100: 21–41 (median ~36). The floor 10/20/30 weekly milestones are reachable, which looks right.
- **Fixed 24 Nov (Claude): floor 21 pacing cliff** (was open). Median seconds per floor at Lv 100: floors 11–19 ~55–70, floor 20 (guardian) 87, **floor 21 180**, floors 22–30 110–190. Lv 50: 32 → 106 at floor 21. `vgHpK`/`vgDmgK` only rise ~4% there. The jump is the band change to **Rimehall** (`VG_BANDS[2]`: burrower/frostcaller/sentinel/shambler), whose base HP is about 2× the Glass Choir band. Wright, Binder, Mind and Harm (pet/DoT professions) ran out the 240 s limit on floor 21 at Lv 100, and Binder and Mind died there at Lv 50. Nothing is stuck or immune, it's just slow. Suggestion: normalise each band's mob HP in `vgMob` (e.g. scale by band-1 average base HP ÷ this band's average), so every floor stays the "short arena floor" the design describes.
  - What it actually was (24 Nov): band HP is only ~1.23x Glass Choir, not 2x. Floor 21 stacks three things: the floor elite was a Frostward Sentinel (x3 HP, shields the whole floor at -60% damage; same on 29, and Riftwarder on 32/37), waves go from 4 to 5 mobs, and the second weekly affix (bulwark +25% HP some weeks) starts. Fix in `game41.js`: `vgBandHpK` (band mob HP scaled down to the first two bands' average, never up), `vgEliteType` (floor elite skips warding and splitting types), and 5-mob waves get 4/5 HP and damage each (`vgCount`). After, Lv 100 Vanguard: floor 21 199 s -> 90 s, floors 21-30 90-130 s; Wright now clears to 30 (was timing out at 21). Mind/Binder still die at various floors (13-21) under the bot: a profession-durability question, not the band.
