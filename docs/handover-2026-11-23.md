## Rules these changes add

1. **Any new saved data needs `EXP_KEYS`.** If you add a localStorage key, add it to `EXP_KEYS` **and** `EXP_OPT` in `src/game20.js`, or CI blocks the deploy. Device settings go in `DEVICE_ONLY` in `tests/check_save_keys.js`, with a reason.
2. **Never put `dustreach_cloud_v1` in `EXP_KEYS`.** It holds the device's cloud code, and an export file must never contain it.
3. **Bank:** `loadBank()` rebuilds the record field by field. A new bank field must be added there, or it is silently dropped on the next write (this is why `tabs` is listed there). Anything that moves an item out of the bank should `delete it.bt`.
4. Don't remove `window.__DR_CLOUD_API` (the test override) or the "no upload with zero characters" guard in `cloudPush`.

## Balance sweep (bot, 23 Nov)

Method: `simChar(prof, cap, rarity)` + `simStage` + `simRunFight` on each solo dungeon's final boss. 14 professions × 11 dungeons × tiers N/H/NM/A3/A5, 770 fights, plus Normal at Common/Uncommon gear.

- **Normal is on target with Uncommon gear**: median 56 s, 0.68× max HP taken. Rare gear: 36 s. Common gear: 96 s, 97% kills. That's healthy.
- **Hard-mode tiers scale cleanly**, with Rare gear: H 50 s, NM 59 s, A3 87 s, A5 104 s, and taken 0.57 → 1.59× max HP. Boss HP ratios match `HM_TIERS` exactly.
- **Fixed:** the Vault was the only outlier (see the table above). After the fix, Rare A5 kills went from 10/14 to 14/14.
- **Open, for you to decide: low-level energy starvation.** Share of the fight with no energy to cast (Normal+NM median):
  - Vanguard: 0.26 at Choir (Lv 18), 0.24 at Stillvault (Lv 28), 0.28 at Foundry (Lv 38)
  - Flux: 0.32 at Mine (Lv 7)
  - Siege: 0.17 at Mine (Lv 7)
  - Everyone is at 0 from Heartforge (Lv 48) up. Probably Weave costs versus the energy pool before mid-levels. Worth a look before adding more low-level content.
- **Stillvault (Queen Ysra) barely changes with tier** (54 s at N → 78 s at A5, while boss HP goes ×2.85): the fight is gated by phase timers, not HP. Damage taken does rise (0.9 → 1.9×). That's fine if it's intended, otherwise the Ascent tiers there are a soft touch.

## Vigil Stair sweep (bot, 23 Nov)

Method: `vgSimClimb(1, 60, 240)` for each profession at Lv 50 and Lv 100, Rare gear.

- **Fixed: crash on death in the Stair.** `game5.js` telegraph loop: a burst that kills the player ends the run, `clearTele()` empties `d.tele` mid-loop, and the next iteration read `undefined.upd` (`TypeError: Cannot read properties of undefined (reading 'upd')`). Reproduced on the old build by putting 3 lethal circles on the player. The loop now skips missing entries and doesn't splice a telegraph that was already cleared. Please keep that guard if you rework the loop.
- **Reached floors:** Lv 50: 17–39 (median ~31). Lv 100: 21–41 (median ~36). The floor 10/20/30 weekly milestones are reachable, which looks right.
- **Open, for you to decide: floor 21 pacing cliff.** Median seconds per floor at Lv 100: floors 11–19 ~55–70, floor 20 (guardian) 87, **floor 21 180**, floors 22–30 110–190. Lv 50: 32 → 106 at floor 21. `vgHpK`/`vgDmgK` only rise ~4% there. The jump is the band change to **Rimehall** (`VG_BANDS[2]`: burrower/frostcaller/sentinel/shambler), whose base HP is about 2× the Glass Choir band. Wright, Binder, Mind and Harm (pet/DoT professions) ran out the 240 s limit on floor 21 at Lv 100, and Binder and Mind died there at Lv 50. Nothing is stuck or immune, it's just slow. Suggestion: normalise each band's mob HP in `vgMob` (e.g. scale by band-1 average base HP ÷ this band's average), so every floor stays the "short arena floor" the design describes.


## Next features: AO ideas Dan picked (23 Nov), in this order

Each one deepens systems that already exist; none needs a new zone. Ship them one build at a time, each with its own smoke-test step and `__dr` hooks listed in AGENTS.md, as with Builds Q–W1.

1. **Symbiants and pocket bosses.** Mobs in each region drop **pattern pieces** (4 per pattern, e.g. Aban/Bhotaar/Chi/Dom style, but with our own names). Assemble a full pattern at the workbench (`QREC`) into a **pocket-boss pattern**. Using it at that region's shrine spawns a solo pocket boss. Reuse `RARES`/dyna boss code, and scale the boss with `hmMode`-style tiers if wanted. Pocket bosses drop **symbiants**: profession-line items that sit in the implant slots (`IMPSLOTS`), with stronger stats than implants but stricter requirements (Weave-style `req`, met via skills). Add them to the Collection log (`P.coll`), to bank types and filters (`bankType`, `BFILT`) and to achievements. Saved data goes into the existing character sheet (`futureFields`). If a new localStorage key is needed, follow the `EXP_KEYS` rule above.
2. **Invasions (world event).** Every so often (game-clock driven), an outpost from `OUTPOSTS` is attacked. A banner and a minimap marker appear, 3 waves of invaders land from a dropship (box-built is fine), then a **General** boss arrives. Rewards: credits, writs, a chance of a pattern piece (ties into idea 1), and an achievement. Reuse the rare-spawn tick (`rrTick`) and dyna spawning. Never trigger inside dungeons, sims (`SIMP`) or the Stair. Add a `__dr` hook to force one for tests.
3. **Research.** A slider in the Skills panel: 0–100% of earned XP is diverted into the selected research line, e.g. +2% damage, +3% max health, -3% Weave cost, +2% energy regen, +1 Matrix, and so on. Each line has 10 ranks, and each rank costs more XP than the last. At the level cap, XP that would otherwise be wasted goes 100% to research automatically (unless the slider says otherwise). This gives level-100 play a use for XP. Hook into `statsFor` the way `mxCapFor` does. Save as `P.rsch` v1.
4. **Init and AggDef.** Add an **Init** stat that shortens Weave cast and recharge times (cap ~-40%), from gear, implants, symbiants and research. Add an **AggDef slider** in the Skills panel, from aggressive (+damage, -defence) to defensive (the reverse), at about ±10%. Keep the slider's effect small so it's a playstyle choice, not a must-have. Include both in `simChar` so balance sims stay honest.
